Scientific Principles & Spatial Modeling Standards

1. Spatial Block Cross-Validation: Standard random cross-validation artificially inflates spatial prospectivity metrics (AUC 0.75–0.81) because neighbouring grid cells share spatial autocorrelation. By utilizing 10 km to 40 km spatial block validation, held-out generalization was rigorously measured (true regional skill: 0.62–0.66 AUC).

2. Presence-Background Formulation: In mineral exploration, unrecorded locations cannot be assumed as zero-mineral barren rock. Grid cells without documented occurrences are treated as background samples under a presence-background learning framework.

3. Separation of Known Belt vs Step-Out Targets: Standard priority maps correlate heavily with proximity to existing operational pits. To provide discovery value, step-out targets were explicitly isolated (≥ 5 km from all known records under Medium confidence), yielding 17 distinct exploration zones driven by surface iron-ratio reflectance.

4. Leakage-Free Temporal Modeling: Operational production forecasting features are constructed strictly using backward-looking windows (1, 7, 30, and 90-day lags) to guarantee zero temporal data leakage.
